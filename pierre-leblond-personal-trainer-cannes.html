Background

Fifteen years of strength and conditioning

Before opening Le Clos in May 2026, Pierre prepared elite athletes in seven sports: golf, tennis, handball, rugby, football, judo and motorcycle racing. They include two European Tour golfers, international and national-level handball players, national-level tennis players and international motorcycle racers.

A private coach in Cannes for ten years, he has built his reputation one session at a time. Some clients have trained with him for seven years, with more than 800 sessions, and many came on recommendation.

The method

Precise technique, knowledge of the body, attentive listening

What working with athletes taught Pierre, he applies to every client at Le Clos: correct movement before heavier loads, exercises chosen for your build and your history, and progress built session by session.

A sound knowledge of anatomy means each exercise is adapted to your body, not the other way round. Listening does the rest: how you feel during the session matters as much as what your coach observes.

Pierre coaches in English and in French.

Passing it on

A team trained to the same standard

A coach educator for five years, Pierre has built the Le Clos team to the same standard. Every coach passes internal assessments run by Pierre before working under the Le Clos name. Your first session is usually with him; after that, you train with Pierre or a coach from the team, with the same rigour.

Holds the BPJEPS, the French state certification in sports coaching.
