The studio

A private gym in Cannes, with your own coach

Le Clos is a private coaching studio at 27 rue Jean Jaurès, a few minutes' walk from the Croisette and rue d'Antibes. It is open by appointment only: no open gym floor, no crowd. For the hour of your session, your coach is entirely dedicated to you.

Every exercise is explained, corrected and adapted to your level, whether you are getting back into training or preparing for a competition. The whole team coaches in English. Changing rooms and showers are cleaned daily; towels and water are provided.

How it starts

A consultation with Pierre

Pierre welcomes you at the studio over tea or coffee. We talk about your goals, your training history, your constraints and any pain or past injury. If you are not in Cannes yet, the consultation takes place by WhatsApp or phone. Complimentary, with no commitment, and a reply the same day.

Only in Cannes for a few days? Send us your dates and we can go straight to booking your sessions.

Your first session

Most often with Pierre, prepared from your consultation.

  1. 01

    We assess how you move

    Mobility, posture, core strength, stability and, if needed, a cardio test.

  2. 02

    We train at your level

    A real session, adjusted as we go. The aim is not to exhaust you, but to show you what your body can do.

  3. 03

    We review it together

    What we observed, your strengths, what to work on and the rhythm we suggest.

Your sessions then continue with Pierre or a coach from the team, with the same method.

Before you come

Practical questions

I have not trained for years.

That is the case for many of our clients when they start. The session begins from your actual level, measured during the assessment, not from a standard programme.

I have back pain or an old injury.

Tell us at the consultation. We work around pain, never through it. If needed, we will ask for your doctor's advice.

Solo or duo: which should I choose?

Solo is entirely focused on you. Duo brings two people together in the same session with one coach: as a couple, with a friend or a colleague.

What should I bring?

Just your training clothes and shoes. Changing rooms and showers are available, and we provide towels and water.

How can I pay?

By card, bank transfer or cash.
