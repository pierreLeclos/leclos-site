A body cannot be reduced to a number on a scale. The precision body composition analysis offers a detailed reading of your body, from its composition to its posture, so you know precisely where you stand today.

Why this analysis

Beyond the number on the scale

Weight alone says little about the reality of a body. Two people of the same weight can have a very different composition: muscle mass, body fat, distribution, balance.

At Le Clos, the precision body composition analysis provides this detailed reading: a clear picture, rather than an estimate by eye.

Five dimensions

What the analysis reveals

  • Composition: muscle mass, body fat, visceral fat, body water, bone mass, basal metabolic rate.
  • Distribution: arms, legs and trunk analysed separately, to reveal imbalances between your right and left side.
  • Posture: a front and side analysis, to identify compensations and points of attention that are hard to see by eye.
  • Cardiovascular markers: resting blood pressure and heart rate, to adjust training intensity safely.
  • Progress: each analysis is kept, so you can measure your progress over time.

How it works

Explained by Pierre, provided in a report

Your results are explained on site by Pierre, the founder of Le Clos: what the figures show, what deserves your attention, and what you can do with it.

What's next

Clarity that guides every decision

This analysis has value in its own right: precise, useful data as soon as your appointment ends. It can also inform coaching at Le Clos if you wish: Pierre and the team then use this data to refine your programme.

It remains optional: it is not required to start coaching.
